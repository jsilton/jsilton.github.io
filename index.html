About me

I enjoy understanding how businesses work, what their customers value and how their teams make progress. Different businesses challenge my assumptions and help me recognize possibilities in new settings.

My career spans agency work, product and growth leadership at Apartments.com, Angi and Redfin, and marketing and sales leadership at Power Support Partners. SEO, paid media and analytics are foundations of my work, alongside building products and developing teams.

Through Silton Strategies, I work with leaders and their teams to improve results and strengthen their ability to make decisions. I want people to understand why we chose a direction, how to tell whether it worked and how to approach the next decision.

I’m originally from Boston and live in Atlanta. Outside work, I’m involved in the theater community, serve as treasurer of the Parkside Foundation and enjoy sailing, soccer, tennis and cooking.
